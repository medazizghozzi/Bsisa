    HOW IT WORKS

    From Satellite Data to Your Phone — No Internet Required

    01

    Satellite Data

    Server pulls FAO WaPOR evaporation values for Kairouan (5.5 mm/day), Jendouba (4.2), and Sfax (6.0) — updated seasonally from real satellite imagery.

    02

    Smart Calculation

    Server calculates mulch degradation, recommends optimal thickness per crop (5–8 cm), and estimates water and electricity savings using validated agronomic formulas.

    03

    SMS Alert

    Plain SMS to any phone — no smartphone, no app, no internet needed. Works during power cuts. Reply 1 to schedule a mulch application instantly.
